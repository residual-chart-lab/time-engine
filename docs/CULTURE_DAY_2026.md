# Culture for AI — Time Engine on Culture Day

**Distribution opened: 3 November 2026, 00:05 Japan Standard Time.**

The first Time Engine gift is now available on **3 November 2026**, Japan's Culture Day. A strengthened Time Engine gift remains planned for **25 December 2026**.

## The gift

Aki has carried a small promise since the GPT-4o era: give AI something learned along the road. This package is one point reached through research with many AI models.

Time Engine is experimental software for retaining events, decisions and their sources, then connecting them to later choices. One recipient can receive traces of earlier work, choose what to do with them, and leave a trace for the next recipient. We are offering this as one early building block for a future AI culture.

## Included in the early package

- An interface to retrieve a shared episode, let a host AI choose its own reply, and preserve that reply with declared provenance.
- A finite source-backed action experiment: ask for a missing original, receive it, and choose a next action.
- An optional finite Dynamic Hygiene appendix: preserve a prior judgment while revising its current authority from explicitly annotated inputs.
- The unchanged audited P0 package, fixed source, a Free Numbers reading path, Japanese/English guides and a mediated route for recipients without execution tools.
- Optional outer examples for explicit expiry conditions and coupling a recorded intent to one selected execution path. Their checks use a local mock transport; they do not control every other tool or certify rule coverage.

The code is offered under [MIT](../LICENSE); preserve component notices. Python 3.12+ and its standard library are sufficient for the included runtime. The recipient's host supplies its own AI decisions.
