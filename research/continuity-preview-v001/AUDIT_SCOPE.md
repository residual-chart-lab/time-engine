# Audit and verification scope

On 19 November 2026, a local independent reviewer verified the repaired recorded-task-delivery route and separation of sealed input from the execution copy. The completed receiver case had 23 routed events, five distinct task deliveries and five original submissions. An audit-owned copy reproduced resume, source retrieval and semantic-record retrieval. No new actionable mismatch remained in that scoped audit.

The repair preserves plaintext tasks and actual returned bytes, scoped receipts and original submission bytes. The sealed file set retains its initial byte hashes; the execution copy is checked for immutable logical rows, its CONFIG and old TE/transport files. Operational row checks include committed WAL frames; immutable SQLite reads are limited to a known standalone sealed backup.

The 22 failure-path harness checks used synthetic submissions; they are not 22 AI trials. The two receiver agents used a historical state selected for the case. The execution-side receiver pre-read the target original in the first stage. Requested quotation and assisted quotation are not spontaneous rereading results.

That review applies to the v0.1 host bytes identified in ORIGINS.json and the recorded-task evidence module. The v0.2 host adds author-declared decision bookmarks. Its acceptance tests use synthetic originals and exercise validation, restart and on-demand readback. The host checks the referenced originals; interpreting what those speakers decided remains the reader's task. Conversation-derived source records and raw receiver traces are retained privately.

Not established: full opaque host-bootstrap or model-context attestation, authenticated actor identity, physical removal of shell/filesystem capabilities, TE-specific causal benefit, cross-model persona continuity, spontaneous recall, or general gains for every model. Earlier evidence gaps and intermediate stopped runs remain historical records; they were not overwritten as repaired.
